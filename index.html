How to play

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a real word. Hit Enter to lock it in.

After each guess, the tiles ignite to show how close you were:

  • The letter is in the word and in the right spot.
  • The letter is in the word, but somewhere else.
  • The letter isn't in the word at all.

Solve the Daily Transmission to chart a new constellation — collected forever in your star chart. Or drift through Deep Space for endless play in 4, 5, or 6 letters.
